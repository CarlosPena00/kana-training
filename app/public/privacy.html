Privacy Policy

Kana Flashcards · last updated 27 August 2026

Kana Flashcards collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no server to send data to. It does not ask for your name, your email address, or any other personal information, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions at all — not even internet access. Every Japanese character it teaches is built into the app itself, so it can run with the network switched off, and it is not technically capable of transmitting anything.

What is stored on your device

The app remembers your last quiz settings — which script, which kana groups, how many cards, the quiz direction, and whether extra attempts are enabled — so you do not have to set them up again. This is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Your quiz results are not saved at all; they exist only while a quiz is on screen.

Third parties

None. The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, and no social media integrations. No third party receives any information about you through this app, because the app sends no information to anyone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
